CARE Finisher

CARE (Core Accessory Robustness Exercises): a short two movement finisher added to the end of every lifting day. One core movement, loaded 3 x 5 or unloaded 3 x 15 alternating by day, plus one shoulder and scapular movement at 2 x 10. Both rotate and swap like any other exercise.

Approach

Split runs one type per day, so it needs All Types and at least 3 days: day 1 straight line, day 2 curve, swerve and zig-zag, day 3 down-up and crawl, day 4 hit and run, day 5 wrestle and run. Day 6 becomes a hybrid mix day. Hit and run and wrestle and run days carry less running distance because of the contact work.
